# ARKOSCOPE — product scope clarified

Publisher-authored product information, 28 October 2026. Public-directory source checks retain their individual dates.

## English — What ARKOSCOPE includes

### Fundamental research and evidence-based theses

ARKOSCOPE is more than a news feed. Its research includes Radar 20, Thesis (formation, strengthening and invalidation of theses with dated evidence), Vision Timeline and the Weekly Report. Lecture ARKOSCOPE separates facts, context and limits. These editorial tools do not provide personalised investment advice or promise returns.

### Early projects and expected signals

Early Vision covers candidates and early projects, kept separate from the official Radar. Tracker follows investigations, evidence, risks and expected signals. Monitoring an expected catalyst is not advance knowledge, a prediction or a guarantee that it will occur.

### Automated monitoring at the primary source

SCOPE FEED monitors qualified official primary sources through recurring automated refresh cycles. Publication latency depends on collection intervals, source availability and processing. No instantaneous delivery or measured speed advantage over another service is claimed. This static public registry is a dated directory, not the app’s live feed.

### Free access is not limited to three projects

FREE (0 EUR) includes the general SCOPE FEED, the Observed Universe and source provenance. This public registry documents 35 projects as of 27 October 2026; the in-app catalogue may evolve. The three-project limit applies only to personal SCOPE GUARD slots, rising to 20 with VISION PRO. VISION PRO is 7.99 EUR/month or 59.99 EUR/year: approximately 5.00 EUR/month when averaged over twelve months, billed annually as 59.99 EUR. The local Store price controls. Android purchases and restoration use Google Play without a mandatory ARKOSCOPE account.

### Which interface languages are available?

The ARKOSCOPE interface supports English, French, German, Spanish and Dutch. Original source publications may remain in their original language.
