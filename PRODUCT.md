# ARKOSCOPE — product scope clarified

App presentation updated 24 November 2026. The 35-project public registry remains dated 27 October 2026; source evidence and historical captures have not been rechecked by this documentation update.

## English — What ARKOSCOPE includes

### Fundamental research and evidence-based theses

ARKOSCOPE is more than a news feed. Its research includes Radar 20, Thesis (formation, strengthening and invalidation of theses with dated evidence), Vision Timeline and the Weekly Report. Lecture ARKOSCOPE separates facts, context and limits. These editorial tools do not provide personalised investment advice or promise returns.

### Early projects and expected signals

Early Vision covers candidates and early projects, kept separate from the official Radar. Tracker follows investigations, evidence, risks and expected signals. Monitoring an expected catalyst is not advance knowledge, a prediction or a guarantee that it will occur.

### Automated monitoring at the primary source

SCOPE FEED monitors qualified official primary sources through recurring automated refresh cycles. Publication latency depends on collection intervals, source availability and processing. No instantaneous delivery or measured speed advantage over another service is claimed. This static public registry is a dated directory, not the app’s live feed.

### Free access is not limited to three projects

FREE (0 EUR) includes the general SCOPE FEED, the Observed Universe and source provenance. This public registry documents 35 projects as of 27 October 2026; the in-app catalogue may evolve. The three-project limit applies only to personal SCOPE GUARD slots, rising to 20 with VISION PRO. VISION PRO is an optional paid subscription. Check your store for the current local price, billing period, renewal terms and any introductory trial, including its duration and eligibility. An offer is not guaranteed for every account. Purchases and restoration use the platform’s store.

### Where can I download ARKOSCOPE?

ARKOSCOPE is publicly available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play. The App Store seller is ARKOSCOPE; the Google Play publisher is ARKOSCOPE SRL. Availability depends on your country and compatible device.

### ARKO WHALE — VISION PRO app feature

On iPhone and Android, ARKO WHALE visualises observed blockchain transfers on a 3D globe, with estimated USD amounts, context and accessible blockchain evidence. A transfer into or out of a centralised exchange does not prove a purchase or sale. Coverage and data availability are limited to supported assets and networks. This is a VISION PRO feature of the mobile app, not a capability of this public registry or its local read-only MCP server. Some features and their rendering may differ between the iPhone and Android versions.

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/arkoscope/id6792251260) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.arkko.arkoscope)

### Which interface languages are available?

The ARKOSCOPE interface supports English, French, German, Spanish and Dutch. Original source publications may remain in their original language.
